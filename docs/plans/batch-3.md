# Batch 3 plan: proven couple-text reels (draft v2, for review)

Status: **DRAFT. Nothing is rendered or queued.** v1 is replaced: its scripts were in-person scenes I
invented. Every script here copies the premise and twist of a specific high-engagement post
(sources in section 3). Only the wording is rewritten, for originality (Instagram demotes reposts)
and so it fits a couple who are texting from different places.

Research: `docs/research/2026-10-02-text-exchange-format.md` (round 2 has the source table).

## 2. Format, copied from the sources

- **Every item is a reel.** You upload and schedule each one in the IG app and add trending audio there; I render them silent.
- **3-6 seconds, a static chat screenshot, the whole conversation on screen from frame 0.** The biggest sources
  (21-23M plays) are 3.1s long and loop. No animation, only a very slow zoom so the video isn't a frozen frame.
- **Chat UI** follows the source's style: WhatsApp-dark for the LDR one, Instagram DM for the rest.
  Text sits inside the safe zone and has no avatar photos.
- **No Ooops branding.** Captions are a short reaction plus 3-4 hashtags, and nothing claims the chats are real.
- **Two people texting from different places, every time.**
- **Audio:** the sources use Bollywood or trending tracks. You pick one per reel at upload; tell me which, so I can log it.
