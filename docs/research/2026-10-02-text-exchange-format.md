# Text-exchange format research (2026-10-02)

Sources: Scrape Creators, pulled 2026-10-02. Recent posts from the user's reference accounts
(@todayyearsold, @1xeraz, @fumbledmemer, 12 each) plus top posts for #couplememes,
#funnytexts and #boyfriendtexts (10 each). Two reel-search calls failed (API error).
Share counts for other accounts are not exposed by the API; we only have them from the
user's screenshots.

## Findings
1. Big meme pages post text exchanges mostly as PHOTO posts with music, not reels.
   All of @1xeraz's last 12 posts are photo posts; the pinned ones have 0.9M-1.8M likes.
   @todayyearsold's photo posts get 13K-143K likes, and its carousels/reels vary.
2. When reels do win, they are short: the top performers run 3.6-15s.
3. Captions are a short reaction ("Big if true", "Son 😭🥀", "I'm seeing things"),
   not a call to action or a question.
4. The top #funnytexts posts are framed as real texts ("Yes this is a real text I had",
   "I can't make this up"). We must NOT claim our scripts are real.
5. Couple-meme hashtags are led by illustrated couples (@bichi.mao: 2.4M views, 6s) and
   husband/wife skits. Indian couple creators post in Hinglish (comments too).
6. Caveat: hashtag "top" results are not a random sample, and n is small.
