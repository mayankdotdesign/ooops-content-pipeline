## What they have in common

1. **Self-aware confessions, mostly from her side.** In 3 of 4, she admits a lovable irrational habit
   ("calling to say I don't want to talk", "re-reading my angry texts", and "babe we need to talk",
   which panics him because he assumes he's in trouble). Viewers share it to say "this is me / this is you". That's
   the same register as the approved script "i hate you / come home early. i hate you".
2. **The text is the whole joke and reads in 1 second.**
   - It's Instagram's native text: plain white sans with a soft shadow, or white text on a white label box with black text.
   - 1-3 lines, centred or near the top, often in quotes ("babe we need to talk").
   - No custom fonts and no animation. It's on screen from frame 0, and it changes at most once per cut.
3. **Amateur phone editing, nothing cinematic.**
   - Either one continuous take (@rhitika1 pacing on a call; @shane_ewing21 laughing behind her phone),
     or hard jump cuts between 3-5 handheld shots (@reececamryn alternates him and her in different rooms).
   - No transitions, no colour grade, slight shake, auto-exposure.
   - One clip shows the "Made with Edits" badge, i.e. edited in Instagram's own Edits app.
4. **Minimal acting.** One expression or one action per shot: pacing on the phone, laughing into the phone,
   a blank stare, sitting stiffly. No dialogue lip-sync and no performance.
5. **Real, ordinary homes.** Normal apartments and bedrooms: a couch, a kitchen island, rumpled bedding. Lived-in but
   not dirty. Ordinary-looking people in their 20s-30s in loungewear or casual clothes.
6. **5-9 seconds.** They're short enough to loop, and the joke lands well before the end.
7. **Captions are a tiny add-on**: "every time hahaha", "Sorry I was just overstimulated 😂",
   "is this just the most comfy? 😂". There are no CTAs and no hashtag walls on-screen.

## The 2 static text posts (shown as 9:16 reels)

1. **The chat screenshot is centred, with black bars above and below** to fill 9:16. It's a screenshot
   dropped into a vertical frame, not a designed layout.
2. **They deliberately look like a screenshot of a screenshot:**
   - Messenger-style bubbles on a pink or purple heart wallpaper.
   - Slight blur and JPEG compression.
   - The top bubble is cut off mid-word ("I love you" is half-cropped).
   - The phone UI (back arrow, camera icon) is faintly visible in the black area.
3. **Real-person grammar and typos** ("Unfriend all girl in your friendlist", "Block all guy who flirts with You",
   "tell him ur wife said u have to"). Lower-case, short bubbles, several bubbles in a row from one sender.
4. **Content:** clingy or possessive-but-cute girlfriend asks (a list of password demands, then "put my pic in your profile");
   and a sweet one (her "come home / we cuddle", then he tells his manager his wife is "cold and lonely").
