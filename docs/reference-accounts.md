## App accounts

### @flamingoappreels: flamingo cards (added 2026-10-02)

- **What:** the IG account of a couples/friends question-card app. 1.1M followers, 6,852 posts, follows 0.
- **Bio:** "Want more questions. The good ones. Download the app 👇" plus a tracked link (go.flamingoscards.com).
  The CTA is the product itself: more of what you just enjoyed.
- **Grid (from the user's screenshot):**
  - One fixed template: a solid-colour card (mostly raspberry red, some blue/purple), a single serif
    question or headline in the centre, and a tiny "flamingo" wordmark at the bottom.
  - Mostly carousels.
  - Pinned posts are list carousels: "3 fun questions to ask your man", "Funny questions to ask your best friend",
    "5 funny questions to ask your boyfriend".
  - The rest are one-question prompts aimed at your partner: "You sure about marrying me? 💍", "Cute or annoying? 🤔",
    "When we're out... sweet or annoying?", "If we became parents 👶", "How to make your crush notice…".
- **Why it works (hypothesis):**
  - Every post is something you send to your partner to answer. The content is the product,
    so sharing it is the natural action.
  - The volume is huge and the look is consistent.
- **Relevance to Ooops:** the same audience (couples). Their posts are a sample of the app experience.
  Ours could be too (the accountability "jar", owning up, small promises).
